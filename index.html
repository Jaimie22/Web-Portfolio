About Me

Hello viewer! I'm Jaimie, an aspiring game developer, aiming to specialise in level design, and a current student at UWS Paisley. My passion for game development started at a young age, and I've been in love with the craft ever since. Welcome to my digital portfolio, where I showcase my projects, share my journey, and connect with fellow enthusiasts. Feel free to explore my work, read my blog posts, and reach out if you'd like to collaborate or discuss game development.

Permit me to give you a brief insight into who Jaimie is, and how we got to this point. I won't give too much life story, but in essence I'm a 37-year-old, a dad of 3, a husband, and for the longest time, I drove buses. A job I had no love for at all. At 33, I decided to change my life, and pursue a career in game development, starting with my first two years in college doing software development. A massive gamble all things considered. But I'm happy that I took the risk, and became a mature student again. It has re-awoken the passion for gaming, and the curiosity of the worlds I still play in as a gamer.
